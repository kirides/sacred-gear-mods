## v0.5.0 (2026-11-27)

- New: shared window templates for other mods: a screen, a group box and a drop-down.
- Changed: the sprite classes moved to their own style sheet that is only loaded by windows that use a sprite; every other window loads a much smaller theme (225 KB down to 37 KB). Needs a current Sacred Gear.

## v0.4.0 (2026-11-25)

- New: the look of the shared on-screen keyboard (dark keys with a thin gold edge, number pad, text line).
- New: the keyboard button of a text field shows only while you play with the controller; icon buttons sit centred next to their field.
- New: floating drop-down lists (.dd-pop) for windows with scrolling lists.

## v0.3.0 (2026-11-25)

- New: selected text in text fields and text boxes has a brown-gold highlight with light text.
- Under the hood: the theme's README gained a Text fields section on the selection colour.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-24)

- New: drop-down buttons in the game's style, with a list of options.
- New: smaller game-style buttons and icon buttons for toolbars, pagers and tabs.
- New: a set of 172 icons that mods can show in their buttons.
- New: a close button for the title bar of windows.
- Fixed: scroll bar arrows and the slider's minus button sit in the right place.
