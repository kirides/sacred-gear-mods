## v0.1.1 (2026-11-19)

- New: A small pill shows the event's progress and time left, movable in the layout editor.
- New: Hover the pill, or hold Alt, for details: your share, reward and reward odds.
- New: The pill glows at 25, 50 and 75 percent, and shows Completed or Failed at the end.
- New: A teal pill shows kills toward the region boss while you stand in that region.
- New: Single player games without a server run events on their own, with waves, gold and items.
- New: In single player, event bosses are tougher creatures with a name, health bar and reward.
