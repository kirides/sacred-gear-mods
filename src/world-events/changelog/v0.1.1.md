## v0.1.1 (2026-11-19)

- New: A small pill shows the event's progress and time left, movable in the layout editor.
- New: Hover the pill, or hold Alt, for details: your share, reward and reward odds.
- New: The pill glows at 25, 50 and 75 percent, and shows Completed or Failed at the end.
- New: Several events, and the progress toward a region's boss, show together in one group.
- New: Without a server, single player and LAN games run events locally, including creature waves.
- New: Event bosses are tougher creatures with a name, a health bar and a reward.
