## v0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- New: Improved shops. Merchants that sell gear sometimes stock a few magic or rare pieces made for your hero's level, on top of what the game stocks.
- New: the finds come when the game fills a merchant with new wares, and stay until the game resets the stock, so reopening a shop shows the same wares. Settings: chance per shop (60 %), most finds (2), lowest and highest quality tier (7 to 14), level against your hero, and an optional chance after the game's own restock (off).
