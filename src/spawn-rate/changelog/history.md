## v1.0.6 (2026-11-26)

- Fixed: extra creatures are level 1 with 100 health on a Sacred Gear older than 0.9.0, which does not copy the level of the original. The mod index now asks for Sacred Gear 0.9.0 or newer, and the log names the problem once if it happens anyway.

## v1.0.5 (2026-11-24)

- Fixed: Switching to a companion no longer multiplies the creatures around you again.

## v1.0.4 (2026-11-21)

- Improved: Extra creatures near you attack you, as creatures the game places do.
- Fixed: Extra creatures keep the faction of the creature they copy, so hostile ones stay hostile.

## v1.0.3 (2026-11-20)

- New: 'Creatures already there' setting, on by default, multiplies creatures from savegames and visited areas.
- Improved: loading a savegame no longer multiplies creatures it already multiplied.
- Improved: the nearest creatures are multiplied first.
- Improved: the status page shows extras from the world and newly spawned ones separately.

## v1.0.1 (2026-11-19)

- Improved: Harmless animals such as rabbits, doves, birds and farm animals are no longer multiplied.

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-19)

- First release: multiplies the creatures the game spawns for you, from 1x up to 100x, in single player.
