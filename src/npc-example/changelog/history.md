## v0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- New: NPC example, a small mod that shows `gear.npc` (Sacred Gear 0.14.0): Nirael the Collector stands near your hero, has a name of her own, talks in dialog pages, opens a window of the mod from her buttons ("Browse pets", "Trade in rare materials") and walks between two places by the game's clock. Click her to talk (the hero walks up first); Ctrl+Shift+M calls her to you. She belongs to the savegame.
