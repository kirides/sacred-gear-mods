## v0.5.1 (2026-11-24)

- Improved: party window buttons have icons, and text buttons use the game's glow style.
- Fixed: a hero's behaviour list opens above everything in the party window.
- New: a close button in the party window's title bar.

## v0.5.0 (2026-11-24)

- New: skill priority arrows in Tactics; the ability at the top of the list is used first.
- New: companions can run away when their health drops below a share you choose.
- New: "Wait here" is a default behaviour choice for all companions.
- New: No combat arts, Combat arts on and All to me buttons for the whole party.
- New: Keep all and Bring all; each hero keeps their tactics between sessions.
- New: ranged companions move to a better spot when their shots keep missing.

## v0.4.0 (2026-11-24)

- New: formations (line or square) facing your nearest enemy, and a spread between companions (setting).
- New: settings to focus the most wounded enemy near you, and to give each companion its own enemy.
- New: protector fighting style: guards you and attacks whatever comes nearest to you.
- New: "Wait here" behaviour: a companion holds its spot and fights what comes close.
- Improved: companions fight back when hit, and rejoin you after caves or when stuck.
- New: portrait size setting, and portraits per row when placed by hand.
