## v0.6.0 (2026-11-28)

- Fixed: the Dwarf's War Cry (Kriegsschrei) now reaches the companions of this mod. The game gives it only to the caster, the hero you play and its own followers (quest and story companions); now the heroes of the party near the caster get the same effect, for as long as it has left. Setting "War Cry reaches the whole party" under Buffs (default on). Needs Sacred Gear 0.19.0.
- Fixed: summons of a companion (the Vampiress's Wolf Call wolves, for example) no longer push the portraits of this mod down. The game counts them as followers and keeps an empty portrait slot for each; the portraits (and the mercenary behaviour) now only count followers that have a portrait of their own.
- Fixed: fallen companions still get up while the hero you play is down (the game removes a fallen companion, with their gear, 25 seconds after the fall), and one companion's failed revive no longer stops the others.
- Added: when a companion suddenly loses worn items or their weapon, the log now records which items vanished and what happened just before (falls, get-ups, control changes, weapon switches), to track the gear loss down.
