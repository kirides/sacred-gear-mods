## v0.4.1 (2026-11-26)

- Fixed: animals without a run animation, such as cows and bulls, now keep up with the hero instead of trailing at walking pace.

## v0.4.0 (2026-11-25)

- New: an orders drop-down for each animal in the Beastmaster window: Aggressive, Defensive, Passive, Wait here (pets: Follow, Wait here), plus To me / All to me.
- New: an animal told to wait stays where it stands, even when you are far away; allies fight what comes near their spot and return to it.
- New: setting Default orders.
- New: animals keep up with you: they run when far behind and are as fast as the hero (setting Animals run to keep up with the hero).
- New: the filter uses the shared on-screen keyboard, shown only for the controller.
- Needs Sacred Gear 0.14.0.

## v0.3.1 (2026-11-25)

- New: the mod manager offers to install the Sacred theme along with this mod (optional).

## v0.3.0 (2026-11-25)

- New: companions' shared buffs also reach your allies, and pets get their speed part.
- New: you can turn these buffs off or scale them in the Mods settings.
- New: a filter box in the Beastmaster window finds animals by name, with an on-screen keyboard.
- Improved: the size buttons in the Beastmaster window are now icons.

## v0.2.0 (2026-11-25)

- New: every animal in the game can come along, from wolves to spiders and crabs.
- Improved: allies take your hero's level, so they grow stronger as you level up.
- New: allies grow bigger every 18 levels, up to level 216.
- New: change each animal's base size with the minus and plus buttons in the window.
- Improved: the window lists animals by kind and shows each ally's level.

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- First release: animals travel with you; allies fight, pets follow and cannot be hurt.
